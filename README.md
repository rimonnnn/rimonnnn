## 🚀 Featured Project

### 🧾 Masiter | مسيطر

**Cross-Platform Business Management Application**

Masiter is a released Flutter application designed to help shops and small businesses manage products, inventory, customers, sales invoices, and outstanding debts in one place.

The application supports Android and web, Arabic and English, light and dark themes, and multiple invoice printing formats.

**Key Features**

* 📦 Product and inventory management.
* 🧾 Sales invoices, purchase history, and PDF invoice printing.
* 💰 Customer balances and outstanding debt tracking.
* 🔔 Low-stock notifications and reminders for outstanding receivables.
* 🤝 Shared business data and account access.
* 🌐 Arabic and English localization with RTL support.
* 🌓 Light and dark themes.
* 📱 Responsive mobile and web interfaces.
* 🖨️ Invoice printing formats: 58mm, 80mm, and A4.

**My Contribution**

Collaborated with a teammate on application development, sharing responsibility across different parts of the application, including UI implementation, state management, data integration, and core business workflows.

**Tech Stack**

`Flutter` `Dart` `Cubit / BLoC` `Firebase Auth` `Cloud Firestore` `Supabase Storage` `GetIt` `GoRouter` `EasyLocalization`
